# AI Law Enforcement Assistant

## Product Requirements Document

## Mission

Create an AI-powered assistant designed to support law enforcement officers by improving documentation, report writing, information organization, and officer workflow.

## Problem

Officers spend significant time completing reports and organizing information after calls. This system is designed to reduce administrative workload while maintaining officer review, accuracy, and accountability.

## Target Users

- Police officers

- Detectives

- Supervisors

- Command staff

- Law enforcement agencies

## Version 1 Goal

Create an AI assistant that allows an officer to provide information through text or voice and generate a structured draft report that the officer can review and approve.

## Core Features

- AI report drafting

- Voice-to-text documentation

- Incident summaries

- Timeline creation

- Evidence organization

- Policy assistance

- Secure department-based information storage

## Important Requirement

The AI assists officers but does not replace officer judgment. All reports and decisions require human review and approval.
